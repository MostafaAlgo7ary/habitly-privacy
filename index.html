Privacy Policy for Habitly

Last updated: November 2026

Habitly ("the app") is developed by Mostafa Algohary. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Data We Collect

Habitly does not require account registration. All habit data you create (names, notes, reminder times, completion history) is stored locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or store this data on any external server.

Advertising

Habitly displays ads provided by Start.io (StartApp). This advertising network may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID) and usage data to display relevant ads. Please review Start.io's privacy policy for details: https://www.start.io/privacy-policy/

Notifications

Habitly uses local device notifications to remind you of your habits. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device and are not sent through any external server.

Permissions

The app requests notification and alarm permissions solely to deliver habit reminders at the time you choose.

Children's Privacy

Habitly is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
